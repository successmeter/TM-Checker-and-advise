Terms, refunds and privacy

Trademark Advisor is a service of Success Meter Pty Ltd.

What you're buying

A written trade mark filing report for the trade mark and the goods and services you entered, produced by software from IP Australia's public data on the date shown in the report. You decide what to file and you file it yourself; we don't file applications for you.

Not legal advice

The report is general information produced by software. It is not legal advice, it is not a clearance search for using the brand, and Trademark Advisor is not a trade marks attorney or a law firm. IP Australia's examiners decide each application on its facts and other traders can oppose it, so no outcome is guaranteed. For advice on your situation, speak to a registered trade marks attorney.

Refund policy

  1. 14-day refund before lodging. If you haven't lodged an application or TM Headstart request based on the report, ask within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.
  2. TM Headstart promise. If, within 30 days of purchase, you file the report's recommended route and goods and services exactly through TM Headstart, and the Headstart assessment is adverse on a ground the report rated Low risk, send us the Headstart letter and we'll refund you in full.
  3. If we can't produce your report, we refund you in full.

To ask for a refund, reply to the email with your report link. These promises are in addition to your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which can't be excluded.

Privacy

We collect your email address, the trade mark, logo (if you upload one), applicant name (if you give one), and the goods and services you enter, to produce your report and send you the link. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us. The trade mark and goods and services are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) and to IP Australia's search to prepare the report. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Ask us to delete your data at any time by replying to your report email.
