The problem

Trade marks are written for lawyers, and lawyers cost money

Start-ups need to know whether a name is worth building on, but the tools and the law aren't written for them.

A$500+

For one professional search

Professional searches commonly start from about A$500 + GST per search, with advice and filing charged on top. That's a lot to spend before you know if a name is worth keeping.

Results only

Search tools list marks, not meaning

Free register searches show you a list of names. They don't tell you which ones matter, why, or what the law says about them.

Form-filling

Lodgement services file, not explain

They lodge the application you give them. Understanding the risks of the name you chose is left to you.

Research you can understand, with honest limits.

  • Research software built on IP Australia's register and guidance

Kind of trade mark

What is your trade mark?

We check the words against the register. A distinctive design can help descriptive words get accepted, but the registration then protects the design and words together, not the words on their own. It doesn't get around an earlier mark with similar words.

Pictures can't be compared automatically, so we check the goods and services and whether the design is likely to be distinctive. Search IP Australia's image search for similar logos before filing.
