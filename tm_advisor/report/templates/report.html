2. Recommended route

{{ r.route.headline }}.

{% for p in r.route_detail %}

{{ p }}

{% endfor %}
Word markProtects the words in any style, font or logo. The broadest protection, but the words themselves must be distinctive and not already taken for similar goods or services.
Composite markProtects a design and words together. A substantial, distinctive design can carry descriptive words; it does not get around an earlier similar mark, and others may still use the words on their own.
Logo markProtects the picture only. Your name is not protected.
